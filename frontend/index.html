Understand the RFP in the language you prefer

This system helps learners read the South African Space Design Competition request-for-proposal and space-settlement terminology in English, isiZulu, Afrikaans, or a combination, while keeping the original English wording available.

Please note: the official competition RFP remains the source of truth. A translation never overrides official instructions.

How to start

  1. Choose isiZulu or Afrikaans in the reader.
  2. Paste text or upload a TXT, PDF or DOCX file.
  3. Read the translation beside the English original.
  4. Search the dictionary for reviewed terms or suggest a translation.
